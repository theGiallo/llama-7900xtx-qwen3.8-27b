## What was found

1. **Fused q4_0 / q8_0 tile attention** (the prototype of the TensorFold-driven work): the tile attention kernel dequantizes K/V into shared memory instead of converting the whole cache to f16 first. Quality-neutral
   (KLD/PPL within one standard error on STRIX and UD-Q4_K_S with q4_0 KV; identical to every digit with q8_0 KV). Still a flag; making it the default is your decision.
2. **`--spec-draft-n-max 3` and an MTP head beat DFlash2 on every quant** at 131072 (Q3_K_XL +23%, plain Q4_K_S +38%, GSQ-RCO +38%, IQ3_XXS +5% at equal n_max, fused); acceptance .74-.88. The 1.37 GB Unsloth head
   (`mtp-Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_0_dynamicV3.0.gguf`, `-md`) works with any quant of Qwen3.8-27B and reproduces STRIX's/Qwopus' own heads. `GSQ-RCO-IQ3_XXS-mtp.gguf` is a complete model, not a head.
3. **AMD driver resets were what made some earlier numbers odd**: slow prefill of IQ3_XXS / plain Q4_K_S (260-550 tok/s) was measured during TDR clusters; re-runs give ~600. The resets stopped when you capped GPU core + VRAM
   clocks at 2850 MHz. Not VRAM (the process held 15-16 GiB flat while it happened). One separate fatal CPU machine check (WHEA 18) rebooted the PC. `notes/amd_driver_crashes_2026-10-02.md`.
4. **DFlash hang** (small final ubatch at depth > ~92k) is a lazy HIP code-object load deadlock; pre-warming the server avoids it (sizes 24/30/32 hang on IQ3_XXS builds even when empty). `bench/decode_investigation/README.md` parts 11-15.
5. **Where a verify round goes** (113k, 4 columns: 66 ms vs 43 ms serial): attention is the largest part; K-quant matmuls cost 1.9x at 4 columns; snapshot copies are ~2%. `notes/tensorfold_analysis_2026-10-02.md`.
6. Notes on the other leads: the 3090 post (`notes/3090_post_analysis.md`), Strata (`notes/strata_analysis.md`), magnitudedev/magnitude (`notes/magnitude_analysis.md`: V4 engine not release-ready, AMD only via Vulkan, claims are Metal/CUDA).

11. **A head does not need its own embedding/output matrices**: tree 4 branch `exp/mtp-share-io` (`llama_model_share_io`) lets a head file without `token_embd`/`output` borrow the target's (0.26 GiB instead of 1.27 GiB; needs `-fit off`). Same speed and acceptance (UD-Q3_K_XL 51.26 tok/s, hash identical) but
    it does not rescue Qwopus at 262144 (the separate draft model still brings its own KV and compute buffers). It is a ~1 GiB saving for every run with a separate head at other contexts.
12. **Operational**: a server that spills out of VRAM or hangs at shutdown can ignore SIGTERM (needed `kill -9`, no TDR followed); one run returned GPU_BUSY because of it. Copying a 15 GB model into WSL takes ~5 min, not 2.

## Added 2026-10-05
13. **The quant format decides the speculative speed** (multi-column verify cost, item 5): UDflat (`Qwen3.8-27B-UDflat.gguf`, UD-Q4_K_S allocation with flat-cost formats + imatrix) has plain-Q4_K_S aggregate quality (77.9 vs 78.5; best same-top-1, 94.33) and STRIX speed: MTP n3 85.6 / 50.9 tok/s at 11k / 111k, +10-15% over UD-Q4_K_S. IQ4_XS requants are faster but lose quality (71.5 with imatrix). RESULTS 13.9, `notes/quant_format_experiments_2026-10-05.md`.
14. **Prefill**: the weight matmul tops out at ~65 TFLOPS for flat-cost formats (rocmfp4 ~52); `-ub` 2048 gives +2-3%; hipBLASLt would give at most +4-5%; deep prefill is attention-bound, so the attention kernel is the only big lever left.
15. **IQ3 sign patch** (`exp/iq3-signs`): bit-identical, +1-2% end to end; upstream-able, not a speed lever.
16. **`run_model.sh`** (TUI / `--no-tui`) launches any model with the best measured settings and Qwen's published sampling (thinking by default); `bench/prompts/` archives every context used.
17. **tool-eval-bench** (the benchmark of Ivan Fioravanti's post): greedy UDflat 91, STRIX 90; thinking-sampling UDflat 86; more runs in progress. Strata on this GPU: not yet run.
